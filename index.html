Analysis, hints and game review

Opening names and ECO codes come from the lichess chess-openings dataset (CC0, public domain). It lists named lines only. It has no win rates or popularity figures, so none are shown, and the counts are how many named lines the dataset holds after that move, not how often it is played. Moves are listed alphabetically.
